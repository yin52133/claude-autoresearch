# Hypothesis Perspectives

Multi-lens reasoning for generating better hypotheses.

Apply these perspectives before committing to a hypothesis.

## The Four Lenses

### Optimist

"Given everything I know, what is the single most impactful change?"

Focus: magnitude of potential improvement
- What would give the biggest metric jump?
- Is there a known high-leverage spot?

### Skeptic

"What could go wrong? What have similar approaches failed on?"

Focus: failure modes
- Cross-check results log for similar attempts
- Why might this approach regress?
- What edge cases might break?

### Historian

"What have past results and lessons taught me?"

Focus: pattern recognition
- What strategy families worked in similar contexts?
- What should be avoided based on lessons?
- Is this a repeat of a failed approach?

### Minimalist

"Is there a simpler version that achieves the same goal?"

Focus: simplicity
- Can the same improvement be achieved with less code?
- Is there a less invasive approach?
- Does this add unnecessary complexity?

## When to Apply

- For obvious, mechanical fixes: skip or apply briefly
- For strategic changes affecting architecture: apply all four
- When stuck: apply skeptic and historian lenses specifically
- After every pivot: apply optimist lens to restart

## Output

After applying lenses, articulate:
1. The chosen hypothesis
2. Why the optimistic lens selected it
3. What the skeptic raised and how it was addressed
4. What history and lessons suggest
5. Whether a simpler alternative exists
